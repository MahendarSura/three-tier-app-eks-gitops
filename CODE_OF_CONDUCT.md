\# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, professional and welcoming environment for everyone who contributes to this project.

This project encourages collaboration, knowledge sharing and constructive discussions around DevOps, Cloud, Kubernetes, Automation and modern software practices.

All contributors, maintainers and users are expected to communicate respectfully and work together in a professional manner.

---

# Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members should:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally
- Provide constructive feedback during discussions and code reviews
- Respect different technical approaches and opinions
- Help others learn and improve
- Focus on improving project quality
- Follow contribution and security guidelines
- Collaborate in a positive and supportive manner


---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Offensive, disrespectful or inappropriate language
- Trolling or intentionally disrupting discussions
- Publishing private information without permission
- Threatening or aggressive behavior
- Unprofessional communication
- Any activity that creates an unsafe or uncomfortable environment


---

# Project Communication

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Code Reviews
- Discussions
- Other official project communication channels


All communication should remain professional and focused on technical improvement.

---

# Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a healthy collaboration environment.

Maintainers may:

- Review and moderate discussions
- Request changes in Pull Requests
- Remove inappropriate content
- Restrict participation when necessary
- Take appropriate action against violations


Actions will be fair, consistent and based on the severity of the situation.

---

# Enforcement Actions

Maintainers may take action depending on the impact and severity of the behavior.

---

## 1. Correction

**Community Impact:**

Minor violations such as inappropriate language, unprofessional communication, or behavior that negatively affects project discussions.

**Action:**

A private warning may be provided explaining the concern and expected improvement.

The contributor may be guided on appropriate communication standards.

---

## 2. Warning

**Community Impact:**

Repeated violations or behavior that affects collaboration, discussions or project workflow.

**Action:**

A formal warning may be issued.

Continued violations may result in temporary restrictions from participating in project discussions or contributions.

---

## 3. Temporary Restriction

**Community Impact:**

Serious violations including repeated harassment, disruptive behavior, or actions affecting the safety and productivity of the community.

**Action:**

Temporary restriction from contributing, commenting, or participating in project communication channels may be applied for a specific period.

---

## 4. Permanent Restriction

**Community Impact:**

A repeated pattern of unacceptable behavior, harassment, threats, or actions that seriously impact the project community.

**Action:**

Permanent removal of contribution privileges and access to project communication spaces may be applied.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately through the repository maintainer contact method.

Reports will be reviewed carefully and handled confidentially.
